**The head bit.** `ctl+0x24` is cleared and rebuilt on EVERY action start by the on-action-start hook, vtable +0x204
`0xd22a14` (its only exit is the `pop` at `0xd22fb4`, after the rebuild; every switch arm reaches `0xd22e68`):
`ctl+0x24 = 0` (`0xd22e9c`); em 4 returns (`0xd22ea8`); em 5 then sets **bit 0** when `breakLevel(e, 5)` (`0x9d36c`)
>= the byte `[[e+0x75f0]+0x64] + 0xd` (questRank `0x3a8430` <= 4) or `+ 0xe` (rank > 4) (`0xd22eac..0xd22f04`),
bit 1 from part 6 against `+9`/`+0xa`, bit 2 from part 0 against `+1`/`+2`. That array is the `.dtp +0x64` break rows,
4 bytes `{part, level at rank <= 4, level at rank > 4, group}`, and row 3 is the head (part 5), level 1 at low/high rank
and **2 at G rank** — `E:\offline\decode\notes\states-em005_00.md` lines 64 / 274 / 359 (their read, not repeated here).
So **bit 0 = "the head is broken"**: one head break below G rank, two at G rank. Bits 1 / 2 only pick the late
hit records (`0x7db70` hit 0x1a / 0x39 at f300 / f350 / f410), not the beam.

**Per-mode looks** (from the arc, `efx/agents/basarios-scratch/shell02/gen_beam_types.py`'s look key):
u 130 = modes 1, 2, 3, 8, 10 (lives 76 / 76 / 130 / 148 / 176) -> `em005_00:1`; u 131 = modes 5, 6, 7, 9, 11 (lives
40 / 40 / 118 / 126 / 140) -> `em005_00:7`; modes 4 and 12 start nothing (ef 999 / -1). Common params
`em005_00_02`: joint 3, length 7200, push -40, offset (0, -30, 80). **A BEAM_TYPES entry carries one mode's life; a
mode that shares the look keeps its own** (sh float 0).

## 5. Method and the positive control (Basarios, same class)

gate and the setup's +4 / +8 / +0x20..+0x28 stores. Dynamic (ROM-run): `beamsA/bh.py` runs the class's **action main**
frame by frame under unicorn (after `init_array`, on the Basarios harness `efx/agents/basarios-scratch/shell02/bharness.py`,
imported read-only), with the real motion query `0x72714` answering the frame gates (b02run.py's frame model), the
setMotion family and `0xb03f8` hooked to record (and to advance the simulated motion), `0x48b884` hooked to log the
setup, and the setup allocator answering memory filled with 0xcd (so a byte the spawner does not write shows as 0xcd).
